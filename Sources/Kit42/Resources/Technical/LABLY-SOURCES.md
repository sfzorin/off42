# Official compliance mark assets

The registry contains 106 IDs: 91 selectable assets and 15 entries withheld from new insertion because their bundled artwork is a placeholder, unverified, misleading, restricted, or lacks reuse permission. The withheld IDs are `CSA`, `PSE`, `ISO_9001`, `Li_ion_Warning`, `Class_III`, `Neutral_N`, `Signal_Common`, `Mains_Powered`, `Protect_From_Heat`, `Sterile`, `MR_Conditional`, `USB_Certified`, `USB_SuperSpeed`, `USB4`, and `Green_Dot`. Reasons are recorded in the registry descriptions and warnings; the notes below document the source-sensitive cases. The selectable count does not imply that a mark is appropriate for a particular product or jurisdiction. The regional download manifest currently contains 11 source-reviewed SVG assets; each page states the applicable-use condition, and the vector itself does not establish conformity.

## ISO symbol references reviewed

The two bundled infrared/ultraviolet warning drawings are not assigned ISO 7010 references in this library. The unrelated W014 and W015 identifiers have been removed; ISO lists W014 for warning about forklifts and other industrial vehicles, and W015 for an overhead load. The bundled open-book “i” instructions graphic is described as “Consult instructions for use” (ISO 7000-1641), rather than the distinct ISO 7010-M002 mandatory-action symbol. References checked 2026-10-10 against the [ISO 7000 database](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/#search/grs/), [ISO 7010 standard page](https://www.iso.org/standard/72424.html), and [IEC 60417 official preview](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/iec_catalog/product/preview/?id=L3B1Yi9wZGYvcHJldmlldy9pbmZvX2llYzYwNDE3e2VkMS4wfWIucGRm).

## Other registry corrections

- `PSE` is withheld from insertion. Japan uses a diamond PSE mark for specified electrical appliances and a circle PSE mark for non-specified appliances; some dual-function products require both. The current METI [Table 14](https://www.meti.go.jp/english/policy/economy/consumer/product_safety/pse_procedure/pdf/pse_regulatory-instructions.pdf) also lists the associated registered conformity assessment body where required, notifying supplier, and product-specific ratings. The PDF is currently indexed but blocked from direct retrieval in this environment. JET's current [PSE application page](https://www.jet.or.jp/en/law/pse/) links form PSE-RE-702, which asks applicants to provide their product marking rather than supplying standalone artwork. The legacy `/marks/pse.svg` has no traceable official source and does not establish both shapes, so it remains available only to render older projects and cannot be inserted in new labels.
- `CSA` is withheld from insertion because no license to redistribute the bundled artwork was established. CSA Group's [Trademark Usage Guidelines](https://www.csagroup.org/documents/testing-and-certification/certification_marks/Trademark-Usage-Guidelines.pdf) state that certified product manufacturers may use applicable certification marks subject to their client agreements and that written approval is required when the planned use does not fall within the listed authorized categories. The legacy `/marks/csa.svg` remains only so older projects continue to render; it is not offered as a downloadable or insertable mark.
- `Green_Dot` is withheld from new insertion because the legacy `/marks/green-dot.svg` has no verified official-artwork provenance. DSD's [downloads page](https://www.gruener-punkt.de/de/downloads) calls its public EPS the company logo and says German use of its trademark, including third-party reproduction in electronic reference databases, is generally tied to a valid contract. The inspected EPS is a 5:1 corporate wordmark combining the two-arrow device with “Der Grüne Punkt”; it is not the standalone packaging symbol and is not converted or presented here as SVG. The German regional guide links the direct owner-hosted EPS under its correct file type, the [2023 packaging-mark guideline](https://www.gruener-punkt.de/fileadmin/Dateien/Downloads/PDFs/231000_Richtlinien_Markenverwendung.pdf), and the [DSD online-label service](https://www.gruener-punkt.de/de/online-label) for covered customers. Existing projects referencing the old path continue to render.
- `RCM` now uses vector paths converted from the official `RCM.eps` supplied in ACMA’s `rcm_images%20zip.zip` (archive SHA-256 `c86e418ac3f96badc7ff59e066ed6d2abd1c5970bdcb6e262353c6aac1e844e5`; EPS SHA-256 `59d0c9400da97326c98968a16bba557a0e4b932cf8c65106fc434d657ac33272`). The conversion used Ghostscript 9.56.0 WASM and Poppler `pdftocairo`; no product dependency or tracing was used. The `/marks/rcm-official.svg` vector has a 239.74 × 177.01 point viewBox and output SHA-256 `48d05d23fa27033974dd9e3a093699eeada9ab72219d8054a2f11951cc628e87`. Source: [ACMA product labelling guidance and downloads](https://www.acma.gov.au/step-5-label-your-product), archive `https://www.acma.gov.au/sites/default/files/2019-07/rcm_images%20zip.zip`. The legacy `/marks/rcm.svg` keeps a square viewBox and centers the vector paths, so older square project frames do not distort the official mark.
- `ISO_9001` is withheld because its asset reproduces the ISO corporate logo. ISO says it does not certify products and restricts use of its logo; see [ISO certification guidance](https://www.iso.org/certification.html) and [ISO name/logo rules](https://www.iso.org/iso-name-and-logo.html).
- `USB_Certified`, `USB_SuperSpeed`, and `USB4` are withheld because the supplied art either lacks the certification/performance context or does not distinguish USB4. USB-IF says its logos require licensing and qualifying products; see [USB-IF logo license](https://www.usb.org/logo-license) and [USB4 product/packaging guidance](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/usb4_language_product_and_packaging_guidelines_final__0.pdf).
- `Protect_From_Heat` is withheld because the bundled drawing combines a radiation trefoil, sun, and box and cannot be verified as the ISO 780 handling symbol.
